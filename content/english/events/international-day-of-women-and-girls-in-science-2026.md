In celebration of the **International Day of Women and Girls in Science 2026**, the **African Institute for Mathematical Sciences Ghana** will mark the occasion as part of the IYQ Closing Ceremony, as the dates coincide.

During a special session led by **Angela Tabiri**, participants will engage in a thought-provoking panel discussion highlighting the contributions and leadership of women in science. The session will also feature a presentation by former GMSP beneficiary **Dorcas Seshie**, a member of the winning team Qualaria at the AIMS QTEdu Global Ceremony Quantathon, showcasing innovation and excellence driven by young African scientists.
