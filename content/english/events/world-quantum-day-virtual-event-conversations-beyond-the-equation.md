World Quantum Day 2025 is here and we’re excited to mark the occasion with a thought-provoking podcast session dedicated to the future of science and innovation!

Join our **Dr. Prince K. Osei**, and **Dr. Angela Tabiri** as they delve into the theme: **“From Quantum Science to Quantum Technology”, **exploring its relevance for Ghana, Africa, and the next generation of STEM leaders.

This engaging conversation will highlight the growing importance of quantum education, real-world applications and efforts in building capacity within this cutting-edge space.

Don’t miss out on the opportunity to gain valuable insights and be inspired!

**Time: 3:00 PM GMT**
