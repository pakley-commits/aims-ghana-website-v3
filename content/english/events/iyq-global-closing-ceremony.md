Join us in Accra for the closing of the International Year of Quantum Science ✨

On 10–11 February 2026, the IYQ Closing Ceremony, *“Celebration and Reflection: The Quantum Legacy,”* will be held at the **Labadi Beach Hotel**, convening the global community to reflect on 100 years of quantum science and explore its future impact on humanity.

The two-day event will feature high-level addresses, forward-looking panels on quantum science and technology, the launch of the UNESCO IYQ 2025 Report and the Global Quantum Initiative, alongside cultural experiences and dedicated sessions spotlighting youth, education, and talent.

Accra, Ghana 10–11 February 2026 Free admission (mandatory pre-registration with UNESCO)
