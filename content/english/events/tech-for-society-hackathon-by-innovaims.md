Build Tech. Create Impact. Shape the Future. This is a three-day innovation hackathon scheduled between 4th – 6th May 2026, bringing together young African talents to design technology-driven solutions to real societal challenges. Participants will collaborate, prototype and pitch solutions with the potential for real-world impact and incubation support.

Focus Areas ● Education Access ● Health Access ● Financial Inclusion ● Smart Cities

Who Can Apply ● AIMS Ghana students & alumni ● Developers, designers & entrepreneurs ● Early-stage innovators passionate about social impact ● Teams or individuals (team formation supported)

Deadline: 31st March 2026
