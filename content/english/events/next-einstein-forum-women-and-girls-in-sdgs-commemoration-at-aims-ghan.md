We are excited to announce the **Women and Girls in SDGs Forum**, scheduled to take place on **October 11, 2024**, at the AIMS Ghana campus, in commemoration of the **International Day of the Girl Child**.

In partnership with the **Next Einstein Forum (NEF)** and **GirledUp Ghana**, and with support from the **Hewlett Foundation**, this event will embrace the United Nations’ theme, **“Girls’ Vision for the Future.”**

The forum will focus on empowering young women by aligning with key Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):

- **SDG 5:** Gender Equality

- **SDG 10:** Reduced Inequalities

- **SDG 17:** Partnerships for the Goals

Join us as we inspire, engage, and amplify the voices of young women, creating pathways for a more equitable and inclusive future.
