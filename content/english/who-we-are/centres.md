The AIMS Centre of Excellence in Cameroon is located in Limbe, a seaside city with its black sand beaches. A few meters from the Centre, the city features its Botanical Garden unveiling an impressive variety of fascinating plants and its Zoological Garden hosting rain forest animals.

## Ghana

The AIMS Centre of Excellence in Ghana is situated at Manet, Spintex, a vibrant area in heart of Accra, within the Greater Accra Region.

Visit Centre

## AMMI

AMMI is a novel fully funded one-year intensive graduate program that provides brilliant young Africans with state-of-the-art training in machine learning and its applications.

## South Africa

The AIMS Centre of Excellence in South Africa is located in Muizenberg, a small seaside suburb of Cape Town and an area of outstanding natural beauty.

## Rwanda
