We celebrate academic excellence, innovation, and the future of science in Africa at the 13th Graduation Ceremony of the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Ghana. This milestone event will honour the achievements of our 2024/25 graduating cohort, who are completing a transformative journey in mathematical sciences and are now poised to drive change across the continent and beyond.

The ceremony will feature inspiring speeches from distinguished guests, including leading scientists, educators, and industry partners. It is a proud moment to recognize the hard work, resilience, and brilliance of our graduates as they join a growing network of AIMS alumni committed to solving Africa’s most pressing challenges through science.
