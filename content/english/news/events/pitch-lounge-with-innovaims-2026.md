*Build Tech. Create Impact. Shape the Future.*

**AIMS Ghana** through [InnovAIMS,](https://aims.edu.gh/innovaims/) invites young innovators to participate in the Pitch Lounge: a dynamic platform designed to spark technology-driven solutions to real societal challenges.

On **9th April 2026** at AIMS Ghana, participants will collaborate in teams, develop prototypes, and pitch bold ideas with real-world impact and potential incubation support.

**Who Should Apply?** AIMS Ghana students and alumni, developers, designers, entrepreneurs, and early-stage innovators passionate about social impact. Individuals and teams (of 4) are welcome, with team formation support available.

**Focus Areas:** Education Access | Health Access | Financial Inclusion | Smart Cities

**What Participants Gain:** Hands-on innovation experience, mentorship from industry experts, the opportunity to pitch before a professional jury, exciting prizes, incubation pathways under InnovAIMS, and valuable ecosystem networking.

**Application Requirements:** Team application (Teams of 4) Pitch deck (PDF)

**Deadline:** 19th March 2026 Apply [HERE](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd4HVi_MNAb-XCmwtSi57AMux6aiU8KYDS81bxhx9oGFQUWqA/viewform)
