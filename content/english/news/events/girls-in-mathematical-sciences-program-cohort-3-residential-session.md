We are excited to announce that the Cohort 3 of the [Girls in Mathematical Sciences Program](https://aims.edu.gh/girls-in-mathematical-sciences-programme/) (GMSP) – 2024 November Residential Activity, will take place from 24th to 30th November on the AIMS Ghana campus.

The GMSP is a mentoring program designed to inspire and empower high school girls from across Ghana, and this final session will bring together over 35 participants from 14 regions of the country.

This residential program aims to strengthen the mathematical foundation of the GMSP beneficiaries through engaging mini-courses on topics such as Mathematical Problem Solving, Quantum Computing, Quantitative Finance, and Computer Programming. It also includes educational excursions and mentorship sessions.

We look forward to welcoming these talented young ladies and support this impactful initiative.
