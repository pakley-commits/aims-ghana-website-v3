AIMS Ghana joined the global community in a virtual celebration of the **World Science Day for Peace and Development**, highlighting the essential role of science in building inclusive, resilient and sustainable societies.

At AIMS Ghana, this celebration holds deep relevance. As a pan-African centre of excellence dedicated to advanced mathematical sciences training, research, and public engagement, AIMS recognizes science as a powerful tool for development and a pathway to peace. The institution’s mission; to equip Africa’s brightest minds with the skills to tackle real-world challenges; speaks directly to the theme of using science for the greater good.

World Science Day provided an important platform to reaffirm AIMS Ghana’s commitment to promoting scientific literacy, nurturing young researchers, and fostering innovation that benefits communities.

Ultimately, the day reinforced AIMS Ghana’s role not just as an academic institution, but as a catalyst for peace, development, and scientific advancement across the continent.
