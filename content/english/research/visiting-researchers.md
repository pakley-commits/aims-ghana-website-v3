## Overview

#### Join a vibrant and collaborative research environment at AIMS Ghana!

We welcome researchers from around the world, from diverse fields and varying levels of expertise, to contribute to transformative research in the Mathematical Sciences. Our Visiting Researcher Program offers a unique opportunity to engage with a dynamic community of scholars and students committed to innovation, learning, and impact.

##

## Research Areas

We invite researchers who do research across a wide range of topics, including (but not limited to): ● Algebra ● Mathematical Physics ● Quantum Computing and Quantum Algorithms ● Quantum Gravity ● Stochastic Analysis and Applications ● AI in Mathematical Science ● Data Science ● Mathematical Biology ● Climate Change Modeling

#### Flexible Research Stays

Whether you are taking a short research break or planning a year-long sabbatical, AIMS Ghana offers flexible hosting options. Visiting researchers may spend a few days, several weeks, or up to one year with us, depending on your availability and goals.

#### Opportunities for Visiting Researchers

- Start new research project

- Collaborate with other researchers from different parts of the world

- Teach advanced courses or lead interactive seminars

- Supervise and mentor graduate student projects

- Present your work at our workshops, seminars, and conferences

- Engage in interdisciplinary discussions and knowledge-sharing sessions

We especially encourage interdisciplinary research that applies mathematical tools to real-world challenges.

##

#### Eligibility

Applicants should hold a PhD in** **the** **Mathematical** **Sciences or a closely related field and should have an active research agenda.

### Support and Resources

Visiting researchers benefit from our 24/7 learning environment, which provides: ● Accommodation and meals ● Office space and high-speed internet ● Research facilities and institutional libraries

For further information or inquiries, please reach out via: **researchvisit@aims.edu.gh**.

### We value your contribution

We invite you to bring your expertise, ideas, and enthusiasm to AIMS Ghana. Be part of a pan-African network committed to shaping the future of Science and using Mathematics and Scientific Innovation for the common good of humanity.
