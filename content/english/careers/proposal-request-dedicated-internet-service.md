The African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Centre of Excellence in Ghana kindly requests your best firm offer for a dedicated symmetric internet connection to its new campus at Spintex in Accra, Ghana. The service is expected to be highly stable and reliable, with overall uptime not lower than 99%. AIMS Ghana intends to establish a rolling 1-year contract with the successful bidder.

The address and GPS coordinates of AIMS Ghana’s new campus are as follows

- Digital Address: GZ-192-3511

- Map: https://ghanapostgps.com/map#GZ1923511

- GPS Coordinates: 5.634202, -0.124170

**Technical Requirements**

- The service provider will provide one STM-1 (155 Mbps) connection to AIMS Ghana’s campus.

- The provided bandwidth must be dedicated, unshared and symmetric (i.e. uplink bandwidth equals downlink bandwidth).

- The connection will be uncompressed, full-duplex and provided over a fibre-optic ground or aerial connection.

- Latency between AIMS Ghana’s campus and the service provider’s network shall not be more than 10 milliseconds. Download PDF for more information.
