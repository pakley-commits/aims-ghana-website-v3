The African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Ghana, a member of the AIMS Network, invites your **Best and Final Offer (BAFO)** in **Ghana Cedis (GHS)** for the provision of catering services to support its academic operations at its campus in Accra, Greater Accra Region, Ghana.

The catering services are required for approximately **70 beneficiaries**, comprising staff, guest lecturers, tutors, and students from across Africa and other parts of the world, for the **2026/2027 academic year**. Please note that the number of beneficiaries is subject to change and may increase or decrease during the contract period.

The successful service provider will be expected to provide a variety of both local and continental meals on a daily basis, comprising:

- Breakfast

- Lunch

- Dinner

- Packed pastries/snacks to be served after dinner

All meals should be accompanied by an appropriate dessert or a serving of fresh fruit.
