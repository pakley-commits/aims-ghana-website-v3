Within the framework of our academic operations in Accra, Greater Accra Region of Ghana, AIMS Ghana would like to request your Best and Final Offer (BAFO) in GHANA CEDIS (GHS) for the following services:

- Type: Provision of Health Insurance Services for AIMS Ghana students at the AIMS Ghana Centre, East Legon Hills for the 2022/2023 academic year. This includes but is not limited to **42 students and 6 tutors.** General Requirements:

- General Health plan packages
- Annual benefits and limits
- Out-patient and In-patient care

2. Health and Safety measures: Provide health talks and health fairs.Equipment requirements: The Service Provider shall provide all equipment, materials and labour required for the service.

Locations: The services shall be provided at facilities within the environs of AIMS Ghana centre.

Delivery schedule: Service to be delivered on both day and night to students and staff for the 2022/2023 academic year.
