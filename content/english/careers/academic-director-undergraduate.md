Reporting to the Centre President, you will spearhead the management of all Undergraduate Academic Programs. You will oversee a roster of students from across Africa under the direction of the Centre President. Additionally, you will manage and contribute to an innovative and internationally-class academic program at the Undergraduate level in Mathematical Sciences and other Sciences.

In close collaboration with the Academic Manager and the Centre President, you will provide guidance to the launch of an established curriculum and give leadership to a diverse team of tutors and world-class resident and visiting lecturers. Furthermore, you will be an active contributor to research and outreach initiatives, collaborate with network wide academic peers to contribute to the ongoing growth of academic offerings and provide academic support to the Centre President during the project proposal writing and fund raising activities or the development of related documentation.

This is a full-time contract opportunity and will be based in Accra, Ghana.
