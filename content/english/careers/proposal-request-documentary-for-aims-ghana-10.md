Within the framework of our academic operations in Accra, Greater Accra Region of Ghana, we would like to request your Best and Final Offer (BAFO) in GHANA CEDIS (GHS) for the following services:

The consultant(s) will work with the communications and the anniversary planning committee to tell the AIMS Ghana Story through engaging and informative audio-visual content. The documentary will be a historiography of the establishment of the AIMS Ghana centre as the third Centre of excellence in the AIMS Global Network and its subsequent rise to UNESCO Category 2 Centre of Excellence.
