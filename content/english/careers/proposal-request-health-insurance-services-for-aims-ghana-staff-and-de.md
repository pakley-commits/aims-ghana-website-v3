Within the framework of our academic operations in Accra, Greater Accra Region of Ghana, African Institute for Mathematical Sciences, Ghana (AIMS, Ghana) a member of the AIMS-NEI network would like to request your Best and Final Offer (BAFO) in GHANA CEDIS (GHS) for the following services:

- Type: Provision of Health Insurance Services for AIMS Ghana staff and their dependants for 2023 beginning 1st February 2023 to 31st January 2024. This includes but is not limited to 18 staff and 30 dependants General Requirements: General Health plan packages Annual benefits and limits Out-patient and In-patient care
- Health and Safety measures: Provide health talks and health fairs.
- Equipment requirements: The Service Provider shall provide all equipment, materials and labour required for the service.
- Locations: The services shall be provided Nationwide.
