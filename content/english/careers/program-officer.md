## **Overview******

It is expected that the Program Officer will form part of the Academic team and will perform all duties required for the smooth operation of AIMS Ghana.

**Responsibilities******

- Assist in the workload of the Academic department of AIMS Ghana by providing assistance and guidance where necessary.

- Act as main point of contact for admissions queries by understanding the Institute’s strategy about recruitment and use this to guide admissions processes and decisions.

- Assist to advise students on program issues and refer on as appropriate

- Maintain records of students during admissions and provide data to academic team.

- Responsible for keeping applicants informed of progress with their admissions request.

- Assist in the production of core programme information, materials, and student handbooks.

- Take responsibility for the creation and maintenance of accurate student records, in both paper and electronic formats, including liaison with the Academic Manager.

- Assist in carrying out logistical planning and budget execution of program activities.

- Performing other duties as required to meet the goals and mission of the Centre and the global centres network
